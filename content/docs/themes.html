Themes and icons

Each of the 31 app icons has a matching colour theme. Pick a theme and an icon separately in Settings; a theme shows a "matches your icon" label when it belongs to the icon you're using.

All 31 themes

Select any swatch to apply it, the same as the theme button in the header.

Pro themes

3 of the 31 — Diamond Ice, Gold VIP and Holographic — are unlocked with a code in Settings. Nothing else in MuffinEMU is locked.

Icons

31 app icons in total. Icon and theme are chosen separately: picking an icon doesn't change your theme, and picking a theme doesn't change your icon.
